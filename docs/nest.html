The Nest

A read-only aggregator of KNP-26 conformant hashlogs. Each committer runs their own sealer and publishes their own log on their own surface — the way RSS federates. No account, no backend, no central store. The Nest fetches each listed log in your browser, validates the §4.01 contract, and renders the state.

No faculty ranking — by rule

Per KNP 6.05 the Nest must not rank committers as prescient or present aggregate display as evidence of foresight. Calibration, where shown, appears only past a thirty-resolved floor and carries its noise caveat on its face. Per 6.06: this page is an aggregator of commitments, not a publisher vouching for content. Sealed commitments are opaque. For revealed content, each Kall is attributed to its source log; material that is unlawful or targets private individuals is removed from display on notice.

Join the federation

Publish a KNP-26 conformant hashlog — the protocol specifies the container, record shape, and mandatory public construction block; kalls.py is a working reference sealer. Host the log anywhere CORS-open (a raw GitHub URL works). Then add yourself to nest_sources.json by pull request. Your seals stay yours; the Nest only reads.

About

What the desk is, what it is not, and how to check its record without trusting whoever keeps it.

What it is

A method for telling foresight from arithmetic: forecasts sealed before their outcomes, adjudicated blind, and scored on a record that keeps its own misses.

The name of the method is Retro-Prescient Audit. The desk is where it is run in public, on its own forecasts.

What it is not

An instrument under public test, not a validated one. Under thirty resolved forecasts a score is noise, and the desk prints it as noise.

Nothing here is classified or privileged, and nothing here is advice.

Who runs it

One person: an auditor applying his profession's evidence discipline to open sources, publishing as NebelKrähe.

The desk names no one. What it offers in place of a name is a record that anyone can recompute.

The machine's part and the operator's

Machine-collated open sources. Deterministic grading. Model-drafted synthesis under citation audit. Resolution and publication are the operator's.

How to check it

Two reference verifiers take the public files and return a verdict. They need the Python standard library, no account, and no material from this desk.

Corrections

Sealed rows are never edited. A correction is a dated entry that keeps the value it supersedes, and the desk's own defects are printed as findings.

Fonts in this directory are self-hosted copies.

Families: IBM Plex Mono, IBM Plex Sans, Spectral, Cormorant Garamond, Cinzel.
All are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1, which permits
redistribution and self-hosting provided this notice accompanies them.

Retrieved from Google Fonts via fontlocal.py. Subsets retained: latin,
latin-ext, cyrillic, cyrillic-ext. Greek and Vietnamese were not retained.

Self-hosted rather than requested from fonts.googleapis.com so that loading
a page on this site does not transmit a visitor's IP address to a third
party as a side effect of rendering.

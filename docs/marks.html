Nebel und Nacht · the fog, answered

The Marks

A heraldry of the shadow world: services legendary and infamous, rendered as marks. Display is not endorsement. Heraldry has carried marks of abatement — dishonor struck across an escutcheon — for eight centuries, and this wall uses the same grammar: the condemned hang barred, in ash, their records printed. Nacht und Nebel was a directive for making human beings vanish into fog. This desk's sigil is a crow that sees through fog. That is the entire argument of this page: the fog was their weapon; seeing through it is ours. So the monsters hang visible — named, dated, unhonored.

Every sigil on this wall is an original abstraction drawn for this page. No service's official insignia is reproduced here, and no iconography of the atrocity apparatus ever will be.

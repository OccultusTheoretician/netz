## 7.06 - THE SWEEP OF 2026-09-07: FOUR OCCUPANCIES, PRINTED UPON DISCOVERY (7.04)

**A - Count binding.** Certificate Transparency's Signed Tree Head (RFC 6962, 2013) binds a log's size into the anchored digest. DECC-26 claims nothing cryptographic; its count-binding is that construction applied to a partially disclosed evidence population.

**B - Field-level audit-evidence commitments.** A design-science proposal of 2026-07-09 (Wright, "The Audit Evidence Problem Public Ledgers Were Supposed to Solve", singulargrit.substack.com) reaches the same architecture as DECC-26 for selective disclosure of accounting fields - per-field salted commitments under one Merkle root anchored on a public medium, an inclusion proof into the workpaper, corrections as linked further commitments rather than erasures - three weeks before DECC-26's first issuance, and draws its own boundary in the same place: a committed field "proves fidelity to the committed record, not fidelity to economic reality", and "Completeness requires population controls." **C - Committed fog of war.** Dark Forest (announced 2020-08-02: zkSNARK-secured hidden coordinates with a validity proof on every move; its announcement names commit-reveal games as the limited prior exception) and Ingonyama, "Cryptographic Fog of War" (2024-03-28, formalising the mechanic) occupy the mechanism FOGWAR uses. What the desk holds is an audit instrument on a known mechanism: a game as a pure function of scenario bytes, rules version, seed and move log; a Python engine and a JavaScript twin held identical by a differential test; a receipt a third party re-executes.

**D - Persona and frame prompting.** Iadisernia and Camassa, "Prompting for Policy: Forecasting Macroeconomic Scenarios with Synthetic LLM Personas" (arXiv 2511.02458; ICAIF 2025; Banca d'Italia): 2,368 personas prompting GPT-4o to replicate the ECB Survey of Professional Forecasters across 50 rounds found no measurable forecasting advantage from persona descriptions, and diverse prompts produced remarkably homogeneous forecasts. This is the reference result for the desk's H6 (frame drift), registered without a stated prior; it enters the registration (Section 15.3) as a reference, not a prediction.

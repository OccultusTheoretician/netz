What this is, mechanically

Strengths are recomputed in this tab from the spine's Factbook personnel strings by the same parser the desk ships: first-figure anchored, year forms stripped, range low-ends inheriting the high end's scale, anything under 500 or over 5,000,000 refused rather than published. The engine is a byte-faithful port of fogsim.py's run_model — 64-bit LCG, Lanchester square, break at 55% — so any run here re-executes from (scenario, rules_version, seed) alone, which is the property the sealed campaigns stand on. Effectiveness is a fixed constant on both sides by design: no readiness, no equipment, no doctrine. That honesty is the feature.

# KRÄHE'S KALLS — THE ABDUCTION LOG

### NebelKrähe · sealed guesses in his own hand, before the outcome

**Separate instrument from KKR.** KKR carries world-outcome forecasts under third-party-adjudicated resolution. Krähe's Kalls carries *abductions* sealed at the moment spoken, resolved by his own judgment at reveal against the stated basis. Different evidentiary class, tagged apart, never cited as KKR's weight.

**Claim:** each Kall was written, salted, and hashed before its outcome was known. The published hash binds the content without revealing it. Reveal is at his sole discretion, per Kall, forever optional; unrevealed Kalls stay dark permanently. The COUNT is committed by this append-only log — the number sealed is public even when contents are not, so withholding is legible and the record cannot be cherry-picked.

**Seal:** `SHA-256( id | timestamp\_utc | statement | resolution\_basis | salt )`. Public = id, timestamp, hash, probability, deadline, status. Private (vault, never pushed) = statement, basis, salt.

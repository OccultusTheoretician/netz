The desk · operational record

Instrument Health

Every served surface of this desk carries a committed cadence in a tracked file, and this page grades the desk against its own commitment — the disciplined comparison of the stated to the operational, pointed at the desk's own tempo. A LATE row is a finding. It is printed here rather than discovered three days later, because an instrument whose staleness is invisible on its own surface is the defect every other page on this site exists to name.

With scripts off this page is an empty frame by design; the record itself is health.json.

Reading the states

Limit, not a disclaimer

This page reads when each surface last moved, not whether what it says is right — content defects belong to the conformance and citation audits. Dates come from the surface's own internal stamp where one exists, from the repository's commit history where it does not, and from file time only as a last resort, printed as untrusted. The cadences are the operator's commitments and can be revised; a revision is a public edit to health_expect.json and rides the commit history like everything else here. The packet-lag check runs only on the operator's machine, because the packets themselves are withheld from this repository — a limitation this desk states rather than papers over.

FOGWAR

W/P FOGWARrules fogwar/1.0, board 0.1 · scenario TWO CAPITALS

A wargame you can audit. Red's dispositions are committed before your first move and opened in the receipt at the end; every game is a pure function of the scenario, the rules, the seed and your orders, so anyone can re-execute it byte for byte. The combat model is a toy and says so; the machinery is the point.

How to play, and what the fog is

You are blue. Each turn you give up to three orders: tap a region you hold, then tap a neighbour to move part of your force there (into empty ground to occupy, into red to attack), or scout a neighbour to learn its strength this turn. End the turn and red, a fixed rule player, answers; fights resolve where both sides stand.

You see the strength of your own regions; the owner of any region next to one you hold; a neighbour's strength only if you scouted it this turn or fought there. Everything else is fog. Red sees under the same rule.

Whoever falls to forty percent of their force in a fight breaks. After ten turns the side holding more value wins; losing every region loses at once.

The commitment does not hide anything from a player who reads their own browser. It binds the record: with the receipt, a third party can check that the fog held exactly what this page says it held.

    Re-performance

    A receipt is the whole game. From it, anyone reproduces the final state and checks the fog.

    1. Download the receipt when a game ends, or drop any receipt into "Verify a receipt" above: this page re-executes the recorded orders against the same seed and compares the final state hash, then recomputes the commitment from the opened fog.
    2. Do the same outside the browser from a clone of the repository: python fogwar.py verify --scenario fogwar_scenario_two_capitals.json --receipt game.json.
    3. Check that the two engines agree on games neither has seen: python test_fogwar_parity.py --games 60 (needs node).
    4. Read the rules that both engines implement; any change to them is a new rules version.

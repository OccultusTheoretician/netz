DECC-26™ · Denominator-Committed Evidence

Verify a sealed disclosure

Drop in a disclosure bundle. This page checks that every disclosed record reopens its commitment and that the disclosed set came from a sealed population of the committed size — so a partial disclosure cannot hide how much was held back. Nothing is uploaded. Verification runs entirely in your browser; this page makes no network requests of any kind. You do not have to trust the party who gave you the bundle, and you do not have to trust this page — read the source, it is all here.

Produced by denom.py disclose or any DECC-26 conformant implementation.
Supply the anchor from wherever the operator published it. If given, it must match the anchor embedded in the bundle — that comparison is what binds the disclosure to a record the operator does not control.

§ 3

The method

The sealed definition →

Most prediction claims are told after the fact, from memory, by the person who benefits from the telling. The audit removes every one of those outs.

01

Sealed before the outcome.

Every projection is hashed into a commitment the day it is issued.

02

Falsifiable as written.

A stranger reading only the statement must be able to say happened or didn't.

03

Blind adjudication.

The adjudicator sees neither forecaster nor stated probability, must cite evidence, and may return ambiguous.

04

The record keeps its misses.

Hits and misses publish together, permanently.

05

Under thirty resolutions, the score is noise.

The ledger says so on its own face until the record is deep enough to mean something.

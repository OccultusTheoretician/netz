FINDING 03 - THE GATE VALIDATES ROWS IN ISOLATION
Class: gate gap, structural. Dated 2026-08-18. Instrument: coherence.py.

WHAT THE DEFECT IS

Every check in validate_projection examines one row against the standard.
Nothing examines a row against the rows already sealed. So the gate cannot
see the one defect that needs no outcome to detect: two claims by the same
forecaster that no single world satisfies.

This is not a forecaster's error being reported. It is the desk's gate
accepting a pair it had no machinery to refuse.

THE RULE THAT WAS MISSING

The weakest defensible coherence rule is monotonicity:

    if claim A's success set is a subset of claim B's success set,
    then P(A) must be <= P(B).

Two forms of subset arise in this ledger. Nested thresholds over one
window: settling above 100 is a subset of settling above 90. And one
threshold over nested windows: reaching a level at least once in 105 days
is a subset of reaching it at least once in a 150-day span containing
those 105 days. Neither form was checked at any point before today.

THE FIRST INSTANCE, PRINTED IN FULL

Arm: manual/fable-5/unattested. Both rows sealed, both open, neither
resolved. Detected 2026-08-18 on the instrument's first run.

  KKR-20260817-57  p = 40   window 2026-08-18 .. 2026-11-30
    "The US 10-year Treasury constant maturity yield published in FRED
     series DGS10 is at or above 5.00 percent for at least one business
     day between 2026-08-18 and 2026-11-30."

  KKR-20260801-24  p = 30   window 2026-08-03 .. 2026-12-31
    "The 10-year US Treasury constant maturity yield closes at or above
     5.00 percent on at least one trading day between 2026-08-03 and
     2026-12-31."

Same instrument, same threshold, same direction, same "at least once"
predicate. The second window strictly contains the first: 2026-08-03 is
earlier than 2026-08-18, and 2026-12-31 is later than 2026-11-30. Every
world in which the first row succeeds is a world in which the second row
succeeds. The forecaster therefore asserted that a strictly weaker claim
is ten points less likely than the claim that implies it. No world
satisfies both numbers.

WHAT IS AND IS NOT BEING CLAIMED

Neither row is wrong. Both may resolve hit and score well, and their Brier
contributions will be unremarkable. That is precisely why this class of
defect survives an accuracy record: scoring measures the relation between
a claim and the world, and coherence measures the relation between claims.
A forecaster can be well calibrated and incoherent at the same time, and
only one of those is visible in a Brier.

Nothing here is scored. Incoherence is reported as its own class and never
folded into accuracy. The rows are not withdrawn, not amended, and not
void: they were sealed as issued and they stand as issued, per 5.07.

WHAT IS NOT CHECKED, STATED PLAINLY

Coherence is checked only where the comparison is mechanical. Of 748 sealed
rows, 55 carry an instrument, a direction, a numeric threshold and a window
the instrument can parse; the remaining 693 are structurally uncomparable
and are dropped rather than guessed at. A row this instrument cannot parse
is not a row it has cleared. Cross-arm disagreement is printed as
measurement and carries no verdict, because a Brier belongs to one
forecaster and two forecasters differing is not an error by either. The
control arm is excluded: its probabilities are assigned from a rate table,
so testing it for reasoning coherence would measure the table.

The guards in the instrument were each added because the unguarded version
produced a false positive against this ledger on 2026-08-18: direction (a
higher bar is easier for a below-claim, and the first version called a
correct pair a violation), instrument (Brent is not WTI; a 10-year yield is
not a 30-year), and window (the same threshold over four days and over
three months are different claims). Those three false positives are the
reason the check is narrow, and the narrowness is disclosed rather than
described as rigour.

REMEDY

coherence.py runs against the sealed ledger, exits non-zero on any
violation, and is wireable into the daily chain. The gate itself remains
row-local; closing that would require validate_projection to read the
ledger at seal time, which is a larger change and is not claimed as done.
Until it is, coherence is caught after sealing and printed, not prevented.

## THE GRADING DISCIPLINE, restated once

A survival is a search result, not a proof — "no located instance" is the strongest honest form, and every claim above names what would falsify it. Adjacent occupancy is printed beside every open claim so the next sweep grades against the same neighbors. A framing that dies here was never a loss: the claims that survive eight adversarial sweeps are the ones worth defending, and the record of what died is why the survivals deserve belief.
